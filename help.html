Popular Questions

When will FLATRO launch?

FLATRO is currently in development and will be launching soon in Kuwait. Sign up for our waitlist to be the first to know when we launch and to get early access to the app.

Is FLATRO free to use?

Yes! FLATRO is completely free for renters to use. You can browse listings, book viewings, and manage your rental search without any fees. Property owners may have different pricing plans which will be announced at launch.

How do digital contracts work?

Our digital contracts are legally binding documents that can be signed electronically through the app. Once both parties agree to the terms, you'll receive a digital signature request. The signed contract is stored securely and can be accessed anytime through your account.

What areas in Kuwait does FLATRO cover?

At launch, FLATRO will cover all major areas in Kuwait City and surrounding governorates. We're continuously expanding our coverage to include more neighborhoods and property types.
