Find answers to the most common questions about FLATRO and how it can help you find your perfect rental property in Kuwait.

General Questions

What is FLATRO?

FLATRO is a mobile-first property rental platform designed specifically for Kuwait. We make it easy to discover, view, and secure rental properties through our iOS app. From browsing listings to signing digital contracts, everything happens seamlessly in one place.

When will FLATRO launch?

We're currently in development and will be launching soon! Sign up for our waitlist on the homepage to be the first to know when we launch and to get early access to the app.

Is FLATRO available on Android?

FLATRO will launch first on iOS (iPhone). An Android version is planned and will be released shortly after our iOS launch. Sign up for our waitlist to be notified when the Android app becomes available.

Is FLATRO free to use?

Yes! FLATRO is completely free for renters. You can browse all listings, book viewings, communicate with property owners, and manage your rental search at no cost. Property owners may have subscription options which will be detailed at launch.

Features & Services

How does the booking system work?

When you find a property you're interested in, you can request a viewing directly through the app. Select your preferred date and time, and the property owner will receive your request. Once confirmed, you'll get a notification with all the details. You can manage all your upcoming viewings from your profile.

What are digital contracts?

Digital contracts allow you to sign your rental agreement electronically through the FLATRO app. No printing, scanning, or in-person meetings required. The contracts are legally binding and comply with Kuwait's electronic signature regulations. Once signed, both parties receive a copy that's securely stored in their accounts.

Are the listings verified?

Yes! We verify all property listings on FLATRO. Our verification process includes confirming the property owner's identity, ensuring accurate property details, and validating photos. This helps ensure you're seeing real properties from legitimate owners.

Security & Privacy

Is my personal information safe?

Absolutely. We take your privacy seriously and use industry-standard encryption to protect your personal data. We never sell your information to third parties, and we only share what's necessary to facilitate your rental transactions. Read our full Privacy Policy for more details.

How are payments secured?

All payments processed through FLATRO use secure, encrypted channels. We partner with trusted payment providers and never store your complete payment information on our servers. All transactions are protected with the latest security protocols.
