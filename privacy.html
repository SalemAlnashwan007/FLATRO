1. Introduction

Welcome to FLATRO. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

By using FLATRO, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information: When you create an account, we collect:

  • Full name and contact information
  • Email address and phone number
  • Civil ID or identification documents (for verification)
  • Profile photo (optional)

Usage Information: We automatically collect:

  • Device information (type, operating system, unique identifiers)
  • Log data (access times, pages viewed, app features used)
  • Location data (with your permission)
  • Search queries and preferences

Transaction Information: For rental transactions, we collect:

  • Payment information (processed securely by our payment partners)
  • Rental history and contract details
  • Communication records with property owners

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain our services
  • Process your rental transactions and viewing bookings
  • Verify your identity and prevent fraud
  • Send you notifications about your bookings and account
  • Improve our app and develop new features
  • Provide customer support
  • Send promotional communications (with your consent)
  • Comply with legal obligations

We never sell your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Information Sharing

We may share your information with:

  • Property Owners: To facilitate viewings and rental transactions
  • Service Providers: Companies that help us operate our platform (payment processors, hosting providers, analytics services)
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Transfers: In connection with any merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

All third parties are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Secure servers with regular security audits
  • Access controls and authentication protocols
  • Regular security training for our team

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but are committed to implementing the best practices in data protection.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services to you
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements

When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing where applicable
  • Object: Object to certain types of data processing

To exercise these rights, please contact us at privacy@flatro.app.

8. Cookies and Tracking

Our mobile app may use:

  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with our app
  • Functional storage: To remember your preferences and settings
  • Performance monitoring: To identify and fix technical issues

You can manage tracking preferences through your device settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. For significant changes, we will provide additional notice through the app or email.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
