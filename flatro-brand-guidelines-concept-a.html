01

The Symbol

The FLATRO Sheltered Plan symbol combines a roof with an interior floor plan — representing both the home as shelter and the living spaces within. This creates an unmistakable property identity.

The Roof

Universal symbol of shelter and home — instantly recognizable.

The Structure

Building walls define the property boundary.

The Floors

Horizontal divisions represent floor levels and rooms.

The Corridor

Vertical pathway shows interior navigation.

Complete

Roof + floor plan = complete home identity.
